- Dual-process coordination superior to single-system approaches
- Transfer learning validated: IEEE 39 → 118 with minimal retraining

### 13.4 Practical Implications (~1.5 pp)

### 13.5 Limitations (~1 pp)
- Modest optimal power flow improvements
- IEEE test systems (not real-world grids)
- Single geographic region (North American data)
- EV penetration limited to 30% (future grids may be higher)

- The future of power systems is intelligent, coordinated, and hardware-validated
- CAPSM is one step toward that future
